# Names and Identity

"Grothendieckian Universe" and its mark — the D6 craft in its glass with seven lit cells — identify this game and
its author, Vincent Liu. This policy explains how the name and the mark may be used, so that players can tell the
authentic game apart from pages that merely talk about it.

**You may, without asking:**

- refer to the game by name when writing about it, reviewing it, teaching with it or streaming it;
- describe an independent project as "inspired by" or "made with ideas from" *Grothendieckian Universe*, with a
  link to https://grothendieck.space/;
- use screenshots and short recordings of your own play, under the rules in [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md) §3.

**Please ask first before:**

- using the name or the mark in the name of a product, project, organisation, domain or social-media account;
- describing anything as "official", "endorsed", "certified" or "the official version" of Grothendieckian Universe;
- using the game's mark, banner or visual identity in your own branding or merchandise.

**Not allowed:** presenting a modified copy of the game as the game.
