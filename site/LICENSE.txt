GROTHENDIECKIAN UNIVERSE

Grothendieckian Universe is a puzzle game made from the seven works of The Grothendieckian Programme by Vincent Liu (WiseMotif.ai). Every level is one idea of a book; every quotation is the book's own words, with its chapter.
